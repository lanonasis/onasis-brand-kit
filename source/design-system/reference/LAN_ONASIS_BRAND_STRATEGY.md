# Lan-Onasis Brand Strategy 2025

## 1. Brand Identity

### Business Overview
**Business Name:** Lan-Onasis
**Type:** SaaS Solutions Provider
**Focus:** Financial Technology & Digital Transformation

### Mission & Vision
**Mission:** Empower businesses with intelligent, secure, and scalable SaaS solutions that drive digital transformation and financial innovation.
**Vision:** To become Africa's leading provider of enterprise-grade SaaS solutions, bridging technological gaps in emerging markets.

### Core Values
- Innovation Excellence
- Security & Trust
- Seamless Integration
- Local Market Understanding
- Sustainable Growth
- User-Centric Design

## 2. Visual Identity

### Logo System
- Primary Logo: Clean, modern design emphasizing technology and trust
- Secondary Logo: Horizontal variant for digital interfaces
- Symbol Mark: Standalone icon for app and favicon use
- Monogram: Minimalist "LO" for micro applications

### Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Navy (#1B365D) - Trust, stability, and professionalism
- Green (#00D4AA) - Innovation, growth, and financial success

**Secondary Colors:**
- Gold (#FFD700) - Premium quality and African prosperity
- Neutral Grey (#F4F4F4) - Clean, modern interfaces

### Typography
**Primary Font:** [Modern Sans-Serif]
- Headlines: Bold weight for impact
- Body: Regular weight for readability
- Interface: Medium weight for UI elements

**Secondary Font:** [Complementary Sans]
- Documentation
- Technical specifications
- Extended reading
